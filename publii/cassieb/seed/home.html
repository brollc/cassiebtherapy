About me

Hello there!

I'm Cassie, a licensed therapist who is endlessly curious about people, relationships, and what makes us who we are. My work is grounded in the belief that our experiences and relationships shape how we see ourselves and move through the world. I'm particularly interested in helping individuals and couples better understand those patterns, make room for the parts of themselves they may have learned to hide, and build deeper, more meaningful connections.

I believe therapy can hold both depth and lightness. It's a place for honesty, vulnerability, and sometimes a little laughter, too. My approach is thoughtful, relational, and collaborative, with room for curiosity, compassion, and the complexities of being human.

How I think about therapy

Therapy

I use the word work intentionally here. Therapy is many things, and effortful is one of them. It is not easy to sit in places inside ourselves and in emotions we try hard to avoid, to look at things we usually turn away from, to talk with our partner about a topic that has somehow, in some unspoken way, become off-limits. It's hard to believe that a process often described with words like healing, holding space, journey, discovery, growth can also be associated with tears, fear, anxiety, sadness, and grief. Ah, the human experience — full of paradoxes and complexity. Thank goodness I love both of those things!

I pull from a few different theories — narrative, psychodynamic, and somatic, all through an attachment framework. Explore how each one might show up in our work together →

A few of my beliefs in therapy:

  • I believe the process of getting to know ourselves, meeting all parts of ourselves and our stories with compassion, and bringing all we are to our relationships in an authentic, intimate way is one of the most rewarding things we will ever do in life.
  • I believe therapy is sacred work.
  • I believe we need humor in life and also the therapy room.
  • I believe that when our relationship with our partner feels secure, loving, and embracing, we can face almost anything in life. When our relationship feels like we're walking on eggshells and covered in tension, the rest of our lives feels like crap too.
  • I believe that people are complex and inherently good.

Good to know

FAQ

Starting therapy brings up a lot of questions — that's more than okay. Here are the ones I hear most often.

Ask your own
How does online therapy work?

All sessions are held online via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform — from wherever you feel most comfortable, anywhere in Minnesota.

How do I know if we're a good fit?

That's exactly what the free 15–20 minute consultation is for. It's a low-pressure conversation to see if my style and approach feel right for you. If not, I'm glad to offer referrals.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Your privacy is protected by law and by my own ethical commitment. The only exceptions are standard legal duties around imminent harm, which we'll discuss in our first session.

Session Cost

My standard fee is $175 for a 50-minute session.

At this time, I have chosen to operate as a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. This wasn't a decision I made lightly. I recognize that insurance coverage can make therapy more accessible and affordable for many people. At the same time, choosing not to work directly with insurance allows for greater flexibility and autonomy in how I practice and how we approach our work together.

I believe therapy should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can comfortably afford it. With that in mind, I reserve a limited number of spaces in my practice for clients seeking a reduced fee through a sliding scale.

If you're interested in a sliding-scale space, or if the cost of therapy feels like a barrier, please reach out. Even if I don't have a reduced-fee opening available, I'm happy to talk through other options or help you explore ways to access support.
