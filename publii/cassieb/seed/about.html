About me

Hello!

I'm Cassie, a licensed therapist (but I'm guessing you already knew that if you made it to this page) practicing in Minnesota. I specialize in individual and couples therapy.

I've always been fascinated by people.

Have you ever been at a holiday dinner and watched a relative do something you'd never expect and wonder, Why do they do that? Chances are if you are human and have been alive for a while, you've asked that question about many people — even yourself. I ask that question all the time. Thankfully for me, there is a whole field dedicated to understanding human behavior. Even more thankfully, the work of therapy exists so we can understand not just human behavior, but all the complex facets that make us human — the stories we carry, the ways we learn to navigate relationships, and the parts of ourselves we sometimes feel we need to hide.

That curiosity is a big part of what drew me to psychotherapy. I'm especially interested in the ways our experiences and relationships shape who we become, how we connect with others, and how we understand ourselves. I love helping individuals and couples explore those layers with curiosity, compassion, and a little humor along the way.

I believe therapy, at its core, is about curiosity. It starts with asking questions, then being brave enough to find the answers we need. My goal for any client I sit with — whether individual or couple — is that you end our time together having more understanding of and compassion for yourself.

To learn more about my approach to therapy and what happens in session, take a look at The Work.
